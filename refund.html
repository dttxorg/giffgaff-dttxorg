02 · 保号码

封号后获取 PAC

PAC 用来把原号码转到另一家英国网络，有效期 30 天。

PAC

Porting Authorisation Code

它是“携号转网授权码”

PAC 不是退款码，也不是新号码。它相当于一把临时钥匙：把这串代码交给新的英国运营商，新运营商就能把你的原 giffgaff 号码转过去。

  1. 1

    向 giffgaff 申请拿到标有“PAC code”的代码和到期日

  2. 2

    交给新运营商在新卡的“Keep my number / Transfer number”页面提交

  3. 3

    等待号码转入通常在一个工作日内完成，原号码随后在新卡上使用

别选错

PAC 和 STAC 完全不同

PAC
保留原号码

把原 giffgaff 号码转到新运营商。想继续使用旧号码就选这个。

STAC
放弃原号码

只结束旧服务，不把旧号码带走；切换后原号码会被注销。

截图下半部分其实是 STAC

因为它写着 STAC code 和 Cancel STAC。蓝色箭头虽然标了“PAC”，箭头指向的仍是 STAC。要保留号码，请返回上方点击 Get a PAC code，直到结果明确写着 PAC code。

03 · 发申请

选择退款话术

话术已按登录账号提交的场景写好，一键复制即可；建议同时附上余额与停用通知截图。

SUBJECTCancellation within 14 days — full refund request
Hello giffgaff Agent, I am writing to cancel my registration and request a refund. My SIM was activated within the last 14 days, so this request is being submitted within your published 14-day cancellation period. As I am submitting this request while signed in, please refer to the account record for the activation date, mobile number, purchases and current balance. Please refund my remaining credit in full. If I purchased a plan, please refund it in full if completely unused, or refund the unused portion proportionately if it has been partly used. Please confirm: 1. the amount being refunded, with a breakdown of credit and plan refund; 2. that it will be returned to the original payment method; 3. the date the refund is initiated and the expected arrival timeframe; and 4. my case/reference number. If the number has not yet been transferred, please issue my PAC and preserve the number while this request is processed. Thank you.
中文总结

这段话是在告诉客服：账号仍处于激活后 14 天取消期内，要求退还剩余余额；套餐完全没用就全退，部分使用就按未使用比例退。同时要求客服确认退款金额、原支付方式、处理日期、到账时间和工单编号，并保留号码所需的 PAC。

为什么更有利：明确激活日期、余额、四项回复要求，并沿用官方退款口径，减少客服来回追问。

SUBJECTRefund of remaining PAYG credit after service deactivation
Hello giffgaff Agent, I am requesting a refund of the remaining Pay As You Go credit on my account following the disabling/deactivation of my service. This request is being made within 30 days of deactivation. Your published help guidance states that a member may contact an agent to request a refund of credit remaining at the time of deactivation. It also confirms that a PAC may be requested through an agent within 30 days of deactivation. As I am submitting this request while signed in, please refer to the account record and existing case history for the mobile number, deactivation date, balance and previous correspondence. Please: 1. preserve and issue my PAC so I can transfer my number; 2. refund all eligible remaining PAYG credit to the original payment method; 3. provide a full balance breakdown, clearly separating paid top-up credit from any bonus/promotional credit and explaining any excluded amount; 4. confirm the card/payment method used for the refund by its type and last four digits; and 5. provide a case/reference number and the expected processing timeframe. If card details must be re-added before you can process the refund, please tell me exactly where to add them and confirm when the account is ready for the refund. Please treat this as a billing request in the first instance. If it cannot be resolved by the agent team, please escalate it through the formal complaints process and retain the original submission date. Thank you.
中文总结

这段话是在停用后 30 天内申请剩余 PAYG 余额退款。要求客服从登录账号里核对停用日期、余额和历史记录，同时提供 PAC、退还符合条件的充值余额、列出赠送余额与不可退金额，并说明退款卡、到账时间和工单编号。

为什么更有利：直接引用当前官方停用退款说明；同时锁定 PAC、余额拆分、原支付方式、case reference 与升级路径。

04 · 客服拒绝后

第一次被拒，不代表结束

不要反复发送同一句。按“要求书面依据 → 正式投诉 → Deadlock / Ombudsman”逐级推进。

官方入口 打开正式投诉页面 ↗
1
要求复核

让客服写明拒绝理由、适用条款和余额计算。

2
正式投诉

已有 agent case 后提交 complaint，索取投诉编号。

3
要求最终立场

仍不解决时索取 deadlock letter，保留向 Communications Ombudsman 提交的权利。

ROUND 1

客服第一次拒绝：要求书面复核

Thank you for your reply. I do not accept the refusal as currently explained, and I am asking for a manual review of the decision. Your published help guidance states that members may contact an agent to request a refund of credit remaining at the time of deactivation. Your refund guidance also states that unused credit may be returned when a member moves their number to another network. Please provide: 1. the exact reason my refund has been refused; 2. the specific term or policy relied upon, including a link or quoted section; 3. my balance immediately before disabling/deactivation; 4. a transaction-level breakdown separating paid top-up credit from bonus/promotional credit; 5. the calculation showing why each amount is refundable or excluded; and 6. a manual review by a billing specialist or senior agent. Please preserve the original submission date shown in the case history and reply within the same case. Thank you.
中文总结

客服第一次拒绝时，用这段要求人工复核。重点是让客服写清楚拒绝退款的具体理由、引用哪条规则、停用前余额是多少、充值余额和赠送余额如何划分，以及每笔金额为什么可退或不可退。

ROUND 2

复核仍被拒：提交正式投诉

Subject: Formal complaint — refusal to refund remaining PAYG credit Hello Member Relations, I am raising a formal complaint following the agent team's refusal to refund the eligible remaining Pay As You Go credit on my disabled/deactivated account. This complaint is being submitted while I am signed in. Please refer to my account details, balance, original refund request and complete agent correspondence already held in the account history. I requested the refund within 30 days of deactivation. I have asked the agent team to identify the applicable term, provide a full balance breakdown and explain the calculation, but the matter remains unresolved. To resolve this complaint, please: 1. conduct an independent review of the agent decision; 2. refund all eligible paid top-up credit to the original payment method; 3. provide the complete balance and transaction breakdown; 4. explain every excluded amount by reference to the applicable term; 5. confirm my PAC/number remains preserved where applicable; and 6. provide a complaint reference number and written outcome. Please retain the original refund-request date when assessing any time limit. I have attached the previous replies, balance evidence, payment records and deactivation notice. Thank you.
中文总结

这是提交给 Member Relations 的正式投诉。内容说明普通客服已经拒绝退款，要求独立复核决定、退还符合条件的充值余额、给出完整计算和条款依据、保留 PAC，并提供正式投诉编号及书面处理结果。

打开正式投诉入口 ↗
ROUND 3

正式投诉仍被拒：索取 Deadlock Letter

Thank you for the complaint outcome. I do not agree that this matter has been resolved. Please keep this complaint open and arrange a final review of the refund decision and balance calculation. If this is giffgaff's final position and no further internal review will be offered, please issue a deadlock letter confirming: 1. giffgaff's final decision; 2. the reasons and terms relied upon; 3. the amount in dispute; 4. the date the complaint was first raised; and 5. my right to refer the matter to the Communications Ombudsman. If the complaint remains unresolved six weeks after it was raised, I intend to submit the complete case record to the Communications Ombudsman. Please respond in writing and retain all account, balance, payment and case records relevant to this complaint. Thank you.
中文总结

正式投诉仍被拒后，用这段表示不接受结案，并要求最后一次复核。如果 giffgaff 坚持最终决定，就要求出具 Deadlock Letter，写明最终立场、拒绝理由、争议金额和投诉日期，以便向 Communications Ombudsman 继续申诉。

三句针对性补充

“已经超过 14 天”

I am not relying solely on the 14-day cancellation period. I am requesting the eligible PAYG credit remaining at deactivation under your published deactivation and refund guidance. Please assess that request separately.

“余额都是赠送的”

Please provide the transaction ledger showing which amounts were paid top-up credit and which were promotional. Please do not classify the entire balance as promotional without a transaction-level breakdown.

“账号停用，所以余额作废”

Please identify the exact term supporting forfeiture and confirm the recorded deactivation date. My request was submitted within 30 days of deactivation, so please reassess it using the account records already available to you.
