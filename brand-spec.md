## Palette and type

- Black `#101114`: urgency, authority, high contrast.
- Yellow `#FFD400`: deadline and primary action.
- Blue `#2850E8`: official links and procedural guidance.
- Mist `#F3F5F7`: neutral instructional surface.
- Type: existing system sans; English scripts use system monospace to distinguish copy-ready content.

## Design direction

Editorial emergency guide: large deadline-led headline, numbered action sequence, sharp-edged content blocks, minimal ornament. Avoid dashboard-like metrics, decorative gradients, and generic floating cards.
